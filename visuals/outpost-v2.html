The loop that runs while you swipe

AlwaysScout finds what's working→Studio makes 10→Queue, 10 ready→You swipe
RightPosts now→Views, comments, sales→Vault fills, base grows
LeftYour reason→Studio remakes it→Back in the queue a few cards later
When the queue drops under 10, the studio starts 10 more. Your reasons also go into the taste notes in Supermemory, so new videos use them too.

What I filled in

1
Queue of 10, not 100A video made before your note can't use it. With 10 ahead, a note shows up within about 10 cards. Compound renders one in about 1 s, so refilling is fast. The number is my call.
2
On the feed, tap the tabs to leaveSideways swipes are yes and no there, so they can't also switch tabs. On Base and You, swiping sideways moves between tabs. Tinder handles its card stack the same way.
3
You on the right, Raid as a button on the baseBase-builder games put the attack button on the home base, and TikTok keeps your profile and stats at the far right.
4
A streak number, not only the calendarWhen GitHub removed its streak counters in 2016 and kept the graph, long streaks got rarer, weekend activity fell, and people chasing 100-day goals quit (Moldon et al., ICSE 2021). The same paper warns some developers had an unhealthy relationship with streaks. Duolingo's two streak freezes stay in.
5
The chart runs on your hoursThe x-axis is time you've spent in the app, not calendar days, so it shows what each hour of yours turned into.

Posting on every right swipe works within these limits. What I still need to check is whether Compound's posting connection can post publicly. That decides whether the demo shows real views.
